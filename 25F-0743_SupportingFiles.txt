----------------------------------------------------------
MAYDAY-404 RULES
1. You are the Chief Routing Officer (CRO). Survive all ticks.
2. One tick = one turn = 30 simulated seconds. You get 2 actions per turn.
3. Viewing radar / manifest / map / pending arrivals is free.
4. Wrong command = -5 credibility (-10 if the Control Tower is degraded).
5. Win: survive every tick with no crash, hijack, cyber breach or war failure.
6. Lose: any of the above, or credibility reaches zero.
7. Credibility: landing +2, hijack +20, cyber +15, war +30, missing +20.
